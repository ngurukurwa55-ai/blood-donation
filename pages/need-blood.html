Blood Requests

Need Blood?

Blood requests must come through verified healthcare organizations. If you or someone you know needs blood, please contact a hospital or blood bank directly.

Important Information

How Blood Requests Work

Understanding the process of requesting blood through authorized channels.

For Healthcare Organizations

If you are a verified healthcare organization, you can submit blood requests through this platform. All requests are reviewed before being made public.

  • Submit requests with blood type and quantity needed
  • Specify urgency level and required date
  • Track request status from submission to fulfillment
  • Connect with registered donors in your area
🏥

Healthcare organizations: Register your organization to start submitting verified blood requests.

For Donors

Registered donors can view verified blood requests and choose to respond to opportunities that match their blood type and location.

  • View active blood requests from verified organizations
  • Filter requests by blood type and location
  • Express interest in responding to a request
  • Receive notifications about matching opportunities

Important Medical Disclaimer

This platform does not replace professional medical advice or emergency services. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately.

Blood transfusion decisions must be made by qualified healthcare professionals using appropriate testing and procedures.
