About Us

Our Mission

Connecting communities through safe, voluntary blood donation. We believe everyone can make a difference.

Our Purpose

Why BloodConnect Exists

We created BloodConnect to make blood donation accessible, understandable, and actionable for everyone.

Our Vision

A world where every patient has access to safe blood when they need it, and every eligible person understands the importance of voluntary donation.

Our Values

  • Trustworthiness: We provide accurate, verified information and connect users with authorized healthcare organizations.
  • Community: We believe in the power of people coming together to support each other.
  • Education: We make medical information accessible and understandable for everyone.
  • Safety: We prioritize safety in every aspect of the platform.
  • Accessibility: We design for everyone, regardless of ability or background.

What We Do

  • Educate: Provide clear, trustworthy information about blood donation and transfusion
  • Connect: Link potential donors with verified donation opportunities
  • Empower: Help people take action and make a difference in their communities
  • Support: Enable healthcare organizations to communicate blood needs effectively

Core Principles

What Guides Us

These principles shape everything we do at BloodConnect.

Safety First

We prioritize safety over convenience. All blood requests must come through verified healthcare organizations.

Education

We provide accurate, understandable medical information so everyone can make informed decisions about blood donation.

Accessibility

We design for everyone. Our platform follows WCAG 2.2 AA standards and works on all devices and browsers.
