Admission Overview

SACOHAS College of Health and Allied Sciences welcomes applications from qualified candidates who are passionate about pursuing careers in health and allied sciences.

Our admissions process is designed to identify students who will succeed in our programs and contribute to the healthcare community.

Application Process

Follow these streamlined steps to apply to SACOHAS

01

Choose Your Program

Explore our academic programs and select the one that aligns with your career goals.

02

Check Requirements

Review the entry requirements for your chosen program to ensure you meet the criteria.

03

Prepare Documents

Gather all required documents, including academic certificates and identification.

04

Submit Application

Complete the application form and submit it with all required documents.

Entry Requirements

Academic Qualifications

Completion of secondary education with passing grades in relevant subjects including Biology, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

English Language

Proficiency in English language for effective participation in academic programs.

Age Requirements

Candidates must meet the minimum age requirement as specified for their chosen program.

Health Requirements

Good physical and mental health status to successfully complete program requirements.

Required Documents

Completed application form
Copies of academic certificates
Birth certificate or ID
Passport-sized photographs
Health certificate
Recommendation letters

Frequently Asked Questions

SACOHAS offers programs in Clinical Medicine, Nursing and Midwifery, Public Health, Pharmacy, Medical Laboratory Sciences, and Health Informatics.

To apply, choose your program, check the requirements, prepare your documents, and submit your application. Contact our admissions office for assistance.

Requirements include secondary education with passing grades in Biology, Chemistry, and Mathematics, English proficiency, and meeting health and age requirements.

The application deadline is March 2026. Please submit your application before the deadline to be considered for admission.

Required documents include your application form, academic certificates, birth certificate or ID, photographs, health certificate, and recommendation letters.
