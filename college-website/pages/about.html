About SACOHAS

College Overview

SACOHAS College of Health and Allied Sciences (SACOHAS) is a premier health education institution in Tanzania. We are committed to providing quality education and professional training in health and allied sciences.

Our programs are designed to prepare students for successful careers in healthcare, equipping them with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to serve their communities effectively.

Our Mission, Vision & Values

🎯 Mission

To provide quality health education and professional training that prepares competent, compassionate, and ethical health professionals.

🌟 Vision

To be a leading institution in health education, recognized for excellence in training health professionals for Tanzania and beyond.
