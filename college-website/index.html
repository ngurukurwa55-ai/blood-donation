About SACOHAS

Excellence in Health Education

SACOHAS College of Health and Allied Sciences is a premier institution in Tanzania dedicated to providing quality health education and professional training. Our programs prepare students for successful careers in healthcare and allied sciences.

We are committed to developing skilled health professionals who will serve their communities with competence, compassion, and integrity.
